What a loft conversion costs

Every house is different, but these ranges give you a starting point. Your quote will be based on a survey of your own loft.

Four ways to convert a loft

Dormer

A box-shaped extension out of the back of the roof. It's the most popular choice and adds the most headroom for the money.

Hip-to-gable

Straightens a sloping side roof to create a full-height wall. It's common on semis and end-terraces.

Mansard

Rebuilds the roof slope at a steep angle for the most space. It usually needs planning permission.

Velux or rooflight

Adds windows into the existing roof with no change to its shape. It's the quickest and cheapest option, if you already have enough height.

Not sure? Choose "Not sure yet" in the form, and your builder will advise you at the survey.

Questions people ask

Is Fairwright really free?

Yes. You never pay Fairwright anything. Our partner builders pay us a fee for each introduction, so the service is free for you.

Who will contact me, and how?

One vetted local loft firm. We'll tell you their name as soon as you submit the form, and they'll usually call within one working day to arrange a survey. You'll also get a confirmation email from us.

Do I need planning permission?

Often not. Many loft conversions on houses in England fall under permitted development, which has limits on size: up to 40 cubic metres of extra roof space for a terraced house and 50 cubic metres for other houses. These rights don't apply to flats or in conservation areas, and some streets have extra restrictions. Your builder will check your property. See the Planning Portal for the full rules.

Do I need building regulations approval?

Yes, all loft conversions do, whether or not they need planning permission. Your builder will normally arrange it.

What if I'm just researching?

That's fine. Choose "Just researching" in the form and we'll send you our free cost guide instead of passing your details to a builder.

Can I change my mind?

Yes, at any time. There's no obligation to accept a quote, and you can ask us to delete your details whenever you like.
