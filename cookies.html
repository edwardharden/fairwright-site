Cookie policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

In short: fairwright.co.uk doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, tracking pixels and device fingerprinting. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

What we use

We don't set any cookies or use similar technologies on this website. In particular:

  • no analytics tools such as Google Analytics
  • no advertising or social media pixels such as the Meta Pixel or Google Ads tags
  • nothing saved in your browser's local storage

Our fonts and images are served from our own website, so loading a page doesn't send your details to font or image services.

If you arrive from one of our adverts, the link may contain campaign tags. We read these from the page address and, if you send an enquiry, save them with it so we know which adverts work. Nothing is stored on your device. Our privacy notice explains how we handle enquiries.

If this changes

If we start using analytics or advertising cookies, we'll ask for your permission before setting any that aren't strictly necessary, give you a simple way to change your mind, and update this policy first.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Because this site doesn't use them, doing so won't affect how it works.
